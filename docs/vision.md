# Product intent and possible directions

## Established project goal

Alpha in AGENTS.md is authoritative: a real client connects, authenticates and manages anime groups
and their child entries. Stable returned IDs, permissions, consistent membership and compilation,
invalid-request safety and successful persistence/restart are acceptance requirements.
Implementation evidence and remaining limitations are summarized in current-state.md.

Favor understandable incremental changes, explicit data semantics and measured performance work.
The product owner chooses priorities and consequential behavior; specialist suggestions do not approve scope.

## Imported ideas requiring confirmation

The Downloads starter kit describes a dark, restrained interface, compact expandable anime cards,
high information density, ranking profiles and richer series navigation. These are recorded here
as possible directions, not verified requirements for this repository or tasks to implement.
Accounts and import tooling already exist in code and are not hypothetical future additions.

Do not infer a frontend framework, public platform, streaming service or microservice architecture
from those ideas. Ask about visual/product preferences when they become material to an authorized task.
